# Sample audio licences

Every sample shipped with Spectrogram is **generated by code in this repository**, not
recorded and not sourced from anywhere else. There is no third-party audio in
this project.

| Sample | Origin | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `senar-gitar` | `data/samples/index.ts` — additive harmonic stack, 196 Hz fundamental | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) |
| `siulan` | `data/samples/index.ts` — exponential sine sweep, 600 → 2400 Hz | CC0 1.0 |
| `desis` | `data/samples/index.ts` — seeded noise through a high-pass and a 6 kHz resonator | CC0 1.0 |
| `akor` | `data/samples/index.ts` — three decaying harmonic stacks, C4/E4/G4 | CC0 1.0 |
| `ketukan` | `data/samples/index.ts` — seeded noise transients with a 90 Hz body | CC0 1.0 |
| `sapuan-nyquist` | `data/samples/index.ts` — linear chirp sampled past fs/2 | CC0 1.0 |

## Why generated rather than recorded

Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **Ground truth.** A visitor can read the generator and know exactly which
   frequencies are in the signal, then check the plate against it. A recording
   would have to be trusted.
2. **No network, no bytes.** The samples are a few hundred lines of arithmetic
   rather than megabytes of audio, and nothing is fetched at runtime (PRD §7).
3. **No licensing question.** Nothing to attribute, nothing to get wrong.

## Rule for anything added later

Never bundle audio without recording its licence in this table. If a real
recording is ever added, it needs the source, the licence, and the attribution
text the licence requires — before it is committed.
